The first release of **Auto Draft**: survey points to a finished drawing.

**Open** the day's survey: CSV, TXT, Excel, or straight from the total station (Leica GSI, Sokkia SDR,
Nikon RAW). Auto Draft joins the lines by their field codes (RE1, WL1, BLD1 CLS, RECT, ARC…), places the
symbols, writes the levels and puts every feature on its own layer.

**Check** before saving: unknown codes, lines with one point, jumps, duplicate numbers and missing
levels are listed; click one to see it, fix a code in one click, undo anything (Ctrl+Z).

**Save**:
- **DXF** for AutoCAD, AutoCAD LT, Civil 3D, BricsCAD and GIS programs, at real coordinates
- **PDF** at scale on the sheet: frame, coordinate grid, legend, north arrow, scale bar, title block
- **KML** for Google Earth
- **Contours** from the ground surface, bending at road edges, banks and drains, every 5th labelled

Sign in once with your free Excel to KML account; your surveys stay on your PC.

**Installing:** download `AutoDraft-Setup.exe` and run it (no administrator rights needed). Windows may
say it protected your PC: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Auto Draft updates itself.
